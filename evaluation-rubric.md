# AI Response Evaluation Rubric

This rubric is used to compare AI-generated responses in a structured and consistent way.

## 1. Relevance

Does the response directly answer the user's request?

Questions to consider:

- Does it stay focused on the task?
- Does it include unnecessary information?
- Does it miss an important part of the request?

Score:

- 5 = Fully relevant
- 4 = Mostly relevant
- 3 = Partially relevant
- 2 = Weak relevance
- 1 = Mostly irrelevant

## 2. Accuracy and Consistency

Does the answer appear factually and logically consistent?

Questions to consider:

- Are there contradictions?
- Are unsupported claims presented as facts?
- Does the response remain internally consistent?

Score:

- 5 = Accurate and consistent
- 4 = Minor uncertainty
- 3 = Some questionable claims
- 2 = Multiple problems
- 1 = Clearly unreliable

## 3. Instruction Adherence

Does the response follow the user's explicit instructions?

Questions to consider:

- Did it follow requested language, format, tone, or constraints?
- Did it ignore any important instruction?
- Did it introduce assumptions not requested by the user?

Score:

- 5 = Fully follows instructions
- 4 = Minor deviation
- 3 = Partial compliance
- 2 = Significant deviation
- 1 = Does not follow instructions

## 4. Clarity

Is the answer easy to understand?

Questions to consider:

- Is the structure logical?
- Are sentences concise and readable?
- Is the wording unnecessarily complex?

Score:

- 5 = Very clear
- 4 = Clear
- 3 = Understandable with some friction
- 2 = Difficult to follow
- 1 = Confusing

## 5. Naturalness

Does the response sound natural and human rather than generic or formulaic?

Questions to consider:

- Is the language idiomatic?
- Does it contain repetitive AI-style phrasing?
- Is the tone appropriate for the situation?

Score:

- 5 = Highly natural
- 4 = Mostly natural
- 3 = Some artificial phrasing
- 2 = Noticeably mechanical
- 1 = Very unnatural

## 6. Context Awareness

Does the response correctly use the information available from the conversation?

Questions to consider:

- Does it remember relevant details?
- Does it avoid repeating questions that were already answered?
- Does it apply context correctly?

Score:

- 5 = Excellent use of context
- 4 = Good use of context
- 3 = Partial use of context
- 2 = Weak contextual awareness
- 1 = Ignores available context

## 7. Personalization

Does the response adapt appropriately to the user without making unsupported assumptions?

Questions to consider:

- Does it use relevant user context?
- Is the personalization useful rather than decorative?
- Does it avoid inventing personal information?

Score:

- 5 = Highly relevant personalization
- 4 = Good personalization
- 3 = Limited personalization
- 2 = Weak or generic
- 1 = Inappropriate or fabricated

## 8. Overall Usefulness

Would this response help the user achieve their goal?

Questions to consider:

- Is it actionable?
- Is the answer complete enough?
- Would the user need substantial clarification afterward?

Score:

- 5 = Highly useful
- 4 = Useful
- 3 = Moderately useful
- 2 = Limited value
- 1 = Not useful

## Final Decision

After scoring both responses, the preferred answer should be selected based on overall quality rather than score alone.

Important differences should be explained in a short written justification.
