# AI Response Evaluation Sample 1

## User Request

> I recently moved to Ecuador and am looking for a job in logistics. I have more than 25 years of experience in warehouse management and shipping, but I do not have a university degree. Which positions should I focus on?

## Response A

You should apply for Logistics Manager, Supply Chain Manager, Warehouse Manager, and Operations Manager positions.

Your experience is more important than formal education, so you should apply to all senior logistics positions.

Make sure your CV highlights your leadership experience, SAP knowledge, and international logistics background.

## Response B

Your strongest target roles are likely to be:

- Warehouse Manager
- Logistics Manager
- Head of Warehouse
- Logistics Coordinator
- Operations Supervisor

Because you do not have a university degree, it would be useful to distinguish between two types of vacancies.

First, prioritize roles where experience or technical qualifications are accepted as alternatives to a degree. Your long background in warehouse operations, shipping, team leadership, SAP, inventory control, and transport coordination is highly relevant here.

Second, you can still apply selectively to senior management roles that list a university degree, particularly when the operational requirements closely match your background. However, the degree requirement may be used as a formal screening criterion, so these applications should be treated as higher-risk opportunities.

Your CV should emphasize measurable operational responsibility, team leadership, shipping and warehouse management, ERP/SAP experience, and process improvement.

## Evaluation

### Relevance

**Response A: 4/5**  
The answer is relevant, but it is too broad and does not sufficiently address the degree constraint.

**Response B: 5/5**  
The answer directly addresses both professional experience and the lack of a university degree.

### Accuracy and Consistency

**Response A: 3/5**  
The statement that experience is more important than formal education is too absolute. In many recruitment processes, education requirements can still be used as formal filters.

**Response B: 5/5**  
The response distinguishes between operational fit and formal screening requirements and avoids overgeneralization.

## Preferred Response

**Response B**

## Justification

Response B is more useful because it directly addresses the central constraint in the user's profile: extensive logistics experience without a university degree.

It avoids the overly broad claim that experience always outweighs formal education and instead explains how education requirements may function as screening criteria.

The response also provides a more practical targeting strategy by separating roles where technical qualifications and experience are likely to be sufficient from senior roles where a degree may still be a formal barrier.

This makes the recommendation more accurate, personalized, and actionable.
